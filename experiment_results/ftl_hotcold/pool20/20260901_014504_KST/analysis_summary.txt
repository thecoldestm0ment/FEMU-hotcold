Pool-ratio observation:
- Both new runs initialized 25 Hot / 103 Cold lines and 24 / 102 free lines after WP allocation.
- Both completed with cold_pool_empty_count=4 and borrow_count=4; no emergency GC.
- Historical V1-P50 had borrow_count=10, while V1-P20 had 4.
- V1-P20 WAF differs from historical V1-P50 by only -0.0171%. Because classifier-reset semantics differ, this is descriptive and does not isolate the pool-ratio effect.

Interpretation:
- 20:80 operated correctly and reduced observed Cold-pool borrowing relative to the historical 50:50 run.
- V4's time-window + erase-feedback design was active, but it classified substantially more writes as Hot and moved far more GC pages through the Hot path.
- In this seed, average copy cost and normalized GC frequency both increased, so V4 did not improve on the simpler V1-P20 classifier.
- The valid conclusion is classifier-design comparison; erase feedback alone was not isolated.
